FAQ

What is Vibe-Coded App Rescue?

An audit-and-fix service for apps built with AI tools like Cursor, Lovable, Bolt, Replit, v0, or Claude Code. It covers deploy and hosting, auth and roles, security, bugs, and performance.

What is the $149 Quick Fix?

One specific bug or deploy issue, fixed and delivered as a pull request within 48 hours. Good when you already know what is broken. If the issue turns out to be bigger than one fix, we tell you before any work starts and quote a Fix Sprint instead.

What does the $99 Health Scan include?

An automated and agent-reviewed audit with a severity-ranked report and evidence, delivered in 48 hours. The $99 is credited toward a Quick Fix, Fix Sprint, or Launch-Ready tier.

How are fixes delivered?

As pull requests to your repository, with a plain-English handover note. You approve and merge; nothing is merged to production without your approval.

Do you need my production credentials?

Access is read-only by default and least-privilege. Credentials are never stored in chat or the repo, and access is revoked at handover.

What if my app needs a full rebuild?

Full rebuilds and migrations are out of scope. The Scan says when a rebuild makes more sense, and those jobs are quoted separately or referred out.
