Who we are

obrainsights.com is run by Obra Insights, operated by Obra Ventures LLC, a California limited liability company. Contact us at info@obrainsights.com about anything in this policy.

What we collect

Contact and scan forms: your name, email, links you submit, and your message. Newsletter: your email and basic engagement data. Purchases: billing details handled by Stripe. Hosting: standard technical logs such as IP address and browser type.

Service providers

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox by email through Resend. The newsletter runs on Beehiiv. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never receive your full card number. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Our email runs on Google Workspace. Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy.

How we use it

To reply to you, deliver and scope services, send the newsletter you signed up for, process payments, and keep the site secure. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention

We keep form and project records as long as needed to provide services and meet legal or tax obligations, then delete them. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time with the link in every issue.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data by emailing info@obrainsights.com. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU), you may have additional rights, and we will honor them.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will post updates here and change the date at the top.
