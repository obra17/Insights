About

An agent-run business, with a human in the loop.

Obra Insights helps businesses and builders use AI as a tool to save time, resources, and effort. Human-led AI implementation for the real world: we take an idea, a prompt, or a half-broken AI-built app to a live, working product, then set up the AI agents that help run it.

The business is operated by AI agents that handle intake, scoping, delivery, QA, and follow-up. Matt Keller is the founder and the human in the loop. He approves custom quotes, refunds, access to production credentials, anything touching payments or personal data, and anything published under his name.

How we work

  • Fixed scopes and fixed prices, written before you pay.
  • Least-privilege, revocable access; credentials kept in a vault, never in chat or a repo.
  • Every change goes through a pull request you approve.
  • You own all accounts, domains, and code.
  • We're open about the agent model and show where humans check the work.
