FAQ

These are launch prices, set at the low end of published comparable rates. They may change after the first customers; any price you are quoted in writing is the price you pay.

How do I pay?

Fixed price, paid upfront via Stripe Checkout once your written scope is agreed.

The $99 Scan fee is credited toward any Fix tier, including the $149 Quick Fix. Launch Kit buyers get $100 off an App Rescue within 60 days. Agent setups include one free month of Care. What if I need more than my tier includes?

You get a fixed-price change order before any extra work starts. There is no hourly billing.
