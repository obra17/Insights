FAQ

What is Obra Insights?

Obra Insights (obrainsights.com) is a productized services business for solo builders that turns an idea, a prompt, or a half-broken vibe-coded app into a live, working product, then sets up AI agents to help run it.

Who does the work?

AI agents handle intake, scoping, delivery, QA, and follow-up. A human on our team approves pricing exceptions, credentials, payments, and final sign-off.

Are prices fixed?

Yes. Every tier has a fixed price and written scope, paid upfront. Work beyond a tier gets a fixed-price change order, never hourly billing.

Who owns the code and accounts?

You do. Customers own all accounts, domains, and code.
